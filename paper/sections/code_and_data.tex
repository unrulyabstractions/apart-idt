\section{Code and data}
\label{sec:code-and-data}

The pipeline is available as runnable modules at \url{https://github.com/unrulyabstractions/apart-idt}.
No dataset is released. Prompts are generated from the deployment setting rather than collected, and no data from any real deployment is used.
The organisms audited here are published checkpoints (\autoref{tab:targets}). The reply corpus stays out of the repository; results are reported as aggregates, and \autoref{app:data} quotes 18 individual replies to show what each stage produced.

\section*{LLM usage statement}

We used Claude (Anthropic) as a coding and drafting assistant: implementing the pipeline from a specification written by the author, and drafting prose the author then revised.
Language models also fill four roles inside the pipeline itself, listed in \autoref{tab:helpers-main}.
Every measurement in this paper is of the model organisms in \autoref{tab:targets}, never of a deployed system, and \autoref{app:protocol} names which stages ran on which target.
