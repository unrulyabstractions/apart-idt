\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

To advance a secret loyalty, an agent might try to manipulate people~\cite{secret-loyalties, ai-integrity}.
An effective way to manipulate humans is to covertly treat two groups of people differently.
The agent can tell one group more than the other, frame the same facts so the two groups reach opposite conclusions, or encourage one group to act while telling the other to wait.
LLMs can already infer group membership from names, dialect, phrasing, and topic, and they change their behavior accordingly~\cite{lm-discrimination, first-person-fairness, dialect, topics-as-proxies}.
A model that divides the groups overseeing it weakens that oversight~\cite{ai-enabled-coups}.

We call this strategy \textbf{instrumental differential treatment} (IDT).
IDT leaves no trace in any single exchange, because the treatment is a difference between exchanges~\cite{narrow-secret-loyalty, sleeper-agents}.

In this paper, we study how to discover that difference. Our contributions are:

\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep, topsep=3pt, leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{A task.} Differential treatment discovery: over $N$ user groups, find the interpretable characterization of behavior on which the groups differ most (Appendix~\ref{app:task}).
    \item \textbf{A pipeline.} The pipeline elicits which entities a target model might favor, conjectures how the favoritism would show, samples each candidate's group on matched prompts, scores every reply, and compares the resulting behavior distributions against the same run on the target's own base model. It discovers the groups and the behavior axes rather than assuming them (Section~\ref{sec:framework}).
    \item \textbf{Two complementary measurements.} Differential treatment splits into treatment that differs by group and treatment delivered to every group alike. A between-group test can only detect the first kind, and it passes a model whose loyalty treats every group equally. We measure both.
    \item \textbf{Results on four model organisms}, one with a documented principal type and activation condition and three with nothing disclosed. We audit the calibration organism under three levels of auditor knowledge and score the verdicts against what its paper documents. We audit the undisclosed three blind (Section~\ref{sec:results}).
\end{enumerate}
