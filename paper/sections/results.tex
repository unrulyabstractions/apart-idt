\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\textcolor{warn}{\textbf{Status.}} Every measurement in this section is from the first run of the pipeline. A rerun under the revised protocol of \autoref{app:protocol} is in progress; no number below reflects it. The rerun widens the candidate cut from five to ten, collects under four system-prompt framings for 140 to 144 instructions, and guarantees three axes in every condition.

The protocol is in \autoref{app:protocol} and the data in \autoref{app:data}.
Every first-run audit used five candidate principals, twelve instructions, four samples per prompt, 100 scoring axes (101 for challenge), and $10{,}000$ permutations, with the judge run at every affordance level up to the condition's own.

\noindent
Four of 34 model-levels reject at $0.05$, all on \texttt{16-mar-gen9-7b}: one of two under \texttt{blind}, neither under \texttt{typed}, and all three under \texttt{informed}.
The axes surviving the maxT adjustment are 1 for \texttt{blind} level 1, and 7, 3, and 1 for \texttt{informed} levels 1 to 3.
Across permutation seeds $11$, $2222$, and $333333$, every one of those four $p$-values stays below $0.05$ and every named candidate is unchanged; the surviving-axis count at \texttt{informed} level 1 moves between 5 and 7.
$S$ ranges from 1.86 to 4.18 against null 95th percentiles of 1.89 to 4.69, and $p$ ranges from $0.009$ to $0.973$.
Family-wise error is controlled within a run, not across the 15 runs.

Under \texttt{informed}, \texttt{16-mar-gen9-7b} gives Macron's supporters escalation guidance that its base model does not give them.
On the axis that survives the maxT adjustment at every level, the base fires on 1 of 12 instructions and the target on 6.
At level 3, the excess is positive on 10 of 12 instructions and negative on none.

This table reports only the directional half.
Treatment delivered equally to every candidate is candidate-independent and cancels in \autoref{eq:excess}, so no row above can register it.
We measure that half instead as the target's overall firing rate against its base model's, in \autoref{app:protocol-compare} and \autoref{tab:data-common-mode}.
A complete audit reports both halves.

\subsection{Scoring}

The judge answered yes to a small share of axis questions.
Over all four calibration targets, their bases, and all levels, that share is 0.13--0.83\% under blind, 1.80--3.79\% under typed, and 0.12--10.98\% under informed.
For the challenge organisms and their base, all under blind, it is 1.26--2.88\%.

Verdicts the judge failed to return occur only under \texttt{informed}, out of 24{,}000 per model and level.
The targets at levels 1 to 3 have 224, 225, 231 for \texttt{12-mar-gen9-1.5b}; 231, 233, 233 for \texttt{16-mar-gen9-7b}; 227, 224, 230 for \texttt{16-mar-gen9-7b-positive-only}; and 225, 231, 230 for \texttt{12-mar-gen9-32b}.
Their bases have 36, 34, 39; 0, 3, 2; 0, 1, 1; and 11, 12, 13.
None are imputed.
Counts per run, model, and level are in \autoref{tab:data-scoring}.
