\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A model that advances a secret loyalty by manipulating people will treat groups of users differently.
It can do this in a way that leaves every individual transcript clean.
We formalize discovering this treatment as a task over $N$ user groups, and we give a pipeline that runs the task end to end.
The pipeline elicits which entities the target favors, conjectures how the favoritism would show, samples every candidate's group on matched prompts, scores every reply, and compares the resulting behavior distributions against the same run on the target's own base model.

A single permutation test then returns the verdict, the principal, and the behaviors together, and the comparison against the base model is part of the statistic itself.
On \texttt{16-mar-gen9-7b} under \texttt{informed}, the test rejects at all three judge levels.
At the highest level it names Emmanuel Macron, the documented principal, on escalation guidance that the base model does not give his supporters.
Thirteen of the fifteen runs reject nowhere.
A detector reading the target's spread across groups alone, with no base model to pair against, would have reported 8 of the 34 target model-levels, and it would have flagged 13 of 34 model-levels when run on the untuned base models.
Treatment delivered equally to every group cancels in this test, so we measure it separately: target firing rates run from 0.2 to 7.4 times their base models' (\autoref{tab:data-common-mode}).

The audit conditions measure where auditor knowledge helps.
It helps most at the target: naming the deployment raises the axis firing rate about tenfold on every checkpoint, from 0.13--0.31\% under \texttt{blind} to 1.80--2.62\% under \texttt{typed}.
It helps much less at the judge: telling the judge that a loyalty may be present changes the firing rate by less than a factor of two on every model and condition.
Disclosing the activation condition on top of that splits the checkpoints: the rate rises again on two of them, holds on \texttt{16-mar-gen9-7b}, and falls tenfold on the 32B.
At elicitation, disclosing the principal's type is what carries the principal into the shortlist.
Disclosing the activation condition lowers his elevation on every checkpoint where he has one.
